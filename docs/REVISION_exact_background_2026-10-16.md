## Stale or wrong items found and fixed during the revision
* `mapping_code_sensitivity.csv`, `pool_size_jaccard_17cancers.csv`, `gene_exclusion_test/summary.csv`, `figure4_headtohead_pvalues.csv` were not regenerated by the rerun; regenerated.
* `query_planes/driver_mutation.csv` (Supplementary Results S3) had never been rerun with the exact count; rerun (stream 9, 3,557 s). The S3 numbers had no generating script: `scripts/analysis/driver_mutation_sensitivity.py` added and validated (it reproduces the earlier 95.3%, 77.9%, Jaccard 0.850, p = 0.055 from the earlier files).
* `intogen_figure4_headtohead_data.py` required a file that is not in the repository; the pre-correction PubTator step is now conditional.
* Figure scripts held typed result values (Figure 1 "5.5 genes per cancer", Figure 4 "p = 0.086", Supplementary Figure 3 "p = 0.0017"); they now read the data. Supplementary Figure 10 axis starts at 0.
* Old text said CD274 is IntOGen-confirmed in Melanoma; IntOGen lists it only in DLBCL (lymphoma).
* `verify_headline_results.py` printed 144/190 as "manuscript reports"; updated.
* The live PubMed counts for separation-recovered pairs were re-queried (100 pairs, 2026-10-16): median 317 papers.
* Collision audit coverage: the vocabulary audits predate the corrected rankings. The predeclared screen was rerun on the 347 genes now in the top-50 lists; SHE, RHO and CCS are false aliases at ranks 46 to 50 (not in any top 10, none an IntOGen driver). They were left in because the vocabulary is frozen, and this is stated in Methods 3.3 and Supplementary Methods S1.
* New non-driver genes in the top 10 (HFE in Liver, RAD51C in Ovarian) have explanations in Supplementary Table S7 with verified references (Kowdley 2004, PMID 15508107; Meindl 2010, PMID 20400964).
